Scheduling Algorithms

Understand how each algorithm works.

01

FCFS

Processes requests in the order they arrive.

02

SSTF

Chooses the request closest to the current head.

03

SCAN

Moves to the disk boundary, then reverses direction.

04

C-SCAN

Moves in one direction and circularly returns.

05

LOOK

Reverses at the last request instead of disk boundary.

06

C-LOOK

Circularly jumps between the extreme requests.
